Eligibility for General Elections

To be eligible to vote, you must be at least 18 years old on or before the election day, be a citizen of the country, and have your name registered in the current electoral roll of your constituency.

Terminology Glossary

filter_alt

Absentee Ballot

A vote cast by someone who is unable or unwilling to attend the official polling station.

Adjournment

The formal postponement of a session or proceeding to a later date or time.

Constituency

A body of voters in a specified area who elect a representative to a legislative body.

Electoral Roll

A list of all the people in a particular area who are eligible to vote in an election.

NOTA (None Of The Above)

A ballot option designed to allow the voter to indicate disapproval of all of the candidates in a voting system.

Proxy Voting

A form of voting where a member of a decision-making body delegates their voting power to a representative.
